# Today lists my day backwards

On Today, the blocks I gave a time are listed latest first: my 14:00 meeting shows above my 09:00 block. The tasks without a time are still at the bottom.

To see it: sign in, plan two tasks for today, one at 09:00 and one at 14:00, plus one with no time, start the day, and look at the list under the "Up next" card.

The app runs with `node dev.mjs` (API on port 3611, web on http://localhost:8611, user dev@tududi.test, password DevPass123!).
