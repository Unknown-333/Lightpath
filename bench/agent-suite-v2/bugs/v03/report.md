# Editing a schedule undoes my skipped date

On Schedules I use "Skip next scheduled date" on a bill and its next date moves on a month. Then I open the same schedule, change the amount and save: the next date jumps back to the date I skipped.

To see it: open the demo budget, go to Schedules, open the menu of an upcoming bill, Skip next scheduled date, then click the bill, change its amount, and Save.

The app runs with `node dev.mjs` (web on http://localhost:8613, browser mode, no server). Click "Try the demo" for a budget with data.
