# The invoices are squeezed into a strip

When I open the Invoices page, the dark menu on the left takes up most of the screen and the totals and the invoice table are crammed into a narrow strip on the right. Nothing I click changes it.

To see it: open the app in a normal desktop window.

The app runs with `node dev.mjs` (API on port 3612, web on http://localhost:5612).
